Called to work in a castle. Need to do an odd job. 

There's a forest clearing and a long garden path to the great hall. 
There are bickering knights in the foyer.
They welcome us along with the maid. Inside a ways, giant curtains have been put up. There sits a field of all sorts of arcade and funfair games, all power less.
We are told there have been issues for a while. I realize a charm was cast a long time ago on the windows that has since worn off, leaving them as magnifying glasses which scorch the cabinets.

I begin repairs by turning a cabinet on and off, it goes into debug mode. I decide to try this with a few more. I get the maid to unlock the one I am on, but the menu, despite showing a connection, doesn't show any information about the internet or updates. I try another, and get even less far. The wifi seems on, so I connect and see if anything is on my phone. 

An earthquake strikes.
